Prospective Sponsors

Sponsors can donate at GoFundMe or contact us at contact@tedxcypressyouth.com.

If you or your business would like to sponsor TEDxCypress Youth, we have several tiers of funding that help promote you or your business at the event.

TEDx conferences attract one of the most promising pools possible of talented, ambitious youth. When you sponsor TEDxCypress Youth, you expose these youth - and their parents, as well as other audience members - to your business and its potential.

All contributors will have the option to appear on this website.

Individual Contributions

Any contributor with a total sum of $50 or more will appear in the pamphlet.

Tier 1

Any contribution of $200 or more will appear on stage, exposing your business to an audience of promising future talent.

Tier 2

Any contribution of $500 or more will appear prominently on stage, emphasizing your business to both live audiences and on video.

Gold

Any contribution of $1000 or more will appear most prominently on stage and in an emphasized section of the pamphlet.

Platinum

Any contribution of $2000 or more will appear on stage, in the pamphlet, and will be thanked by moderators during the conference.
