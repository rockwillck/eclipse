Prospective Sponsors

Sponsors can donate at GoFundMe or contact us at contact@tedxcypressyouth.com.

If you or your business would like to sponsor TEDxCypress Youth, we have several tiers of funding that help promote you or your business at the event.

TEDx conferences attract one of the most promising pools possible of talented, ambitious youth. When you sponsor TEDxCypress Youth, you expose these youth - and their parents, as well as other audience members - to your business and its potential.

All contributors will have the option to appear on this website.

All Tiers include the benefits of lower tiers.

Tier 1

Any contribution of $50 or more will be thanked on stage.

Tier 2

Any contribution of $150 or more will appear on a slide on stage.

Tier 3

Any contribution of $250 or more will be included in pamphlets and have prominent placement on a slide on stage.

Platinum Sponsor

Any contribution of $400 or more will be featured on the first slide and the last slide, as well as having prominent placement in pamphlets.

Diamond Sponsor

Any contribution of $500 or more will have a logo and message of their choosing placed on every table.
