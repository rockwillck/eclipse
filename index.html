Meaningful ideas worth sharing, presented by youth speakers and passionate educators.

TEDxCypress Youth is an upcoming event founded, organized, and presented by students in Cypress, Texas. Speakers will talk about everything and anything - we want to give youth voices a platform, no matter what those voices say.

What is TEDx?

In the spirit of discovering and spreading ideas, TED has created a program called TEDx. TEDx is a program of local, self-organized events that bring people together to share a TED-like experience. You can learn more about the program here.
